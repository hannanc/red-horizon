RED HORIZON

A classic isometric real-time strategy skirmish. You command the Allied base in the west. The Soviet AI holds the east and will come for you.

  • Deploy: Select your Construction Vehicle and press D (or click it again) to unfold your Construction Hub.
  • Economy: Build an Ore Refinery — its Miner harvests golden ore and returns it for credits.
  • Power: Keep power positive or production slows and defenses shut down.
  • Sidebar: Click a picture to start building. Structures show READY — then click the map to place them (near your base).
  • Army: Barracks trains infantry, the Vehicle Factory builds tanks. Select with left-click / drag, command with right-click.
  • Transports: Right-click a friendly IFV or Halftrack with infantry selected to board. Click the transport again (or press D) to unload.
  • Engineers: Right-click an enemy structure to capture it, or one of yours to repair it fully. The engineer is used up.
  • Win: Destroy every Soviet structure. Lose all of yours and it's over.
